How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Cats, then tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the panel. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Permanent items (cat packs, outfit packs and revive upgrades) come back automatically.

Why didn't my revives or tip come back?

Saved revives and tips are one-time purchases, and Apple doesn't restore those on a new device. Saved revives stay on the device where you bought them. If something went wrong with a purchase, email me and I'll do my best to help. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do saved revives work?

Each revive purchase gives you 3 revives. When you crash, you can use one to keep going, and you can keep using them in the same level until they run out. Any you don't use are saved for later.

How do I unlock more cats?

Clear levels! Every level you beat unlocks a new cat. A random photo cat unlocks at each of levels 15, 20, 25, 27, 29 and 30, or you can get all six at once with the photo cat pack. There's also a secret cat for players who finish #1 on the daily leaderboard 3 times.

How do I play Classic mode?

Classic mode unlocks after you clear level 10.

How do I use my cat's power?

Photo cats have special powers. Double-tap the screen (or tap the power button) when the power is ready.

Leaderboards and trophies aren't showing up

Leaderboards and trophies use Game Center. Sign in under Settings > Game Center on your device, then reopen the app.

Do outfits change gameplay?

No. Outfits are just for fun and look great on every cat.

How do I see the welcome screen again or turn off sound?

Tap Settings in the game's main menu.
