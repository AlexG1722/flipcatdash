Privacy Policy

FlipCat Dash · Effective November 13, 2026

FlipCat Dash is a game made by Alex Gleyzer. Your privacy matters, and this game is built to collect as little as possible.

The short version

FlipCat Dash does not ask for your name, email, or contacts, and does not sell your personal information. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads and Google Analytics for Firebase to see how players progress through levels. These services collect some device and usage information, described below.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you choose to watch an ad (for example, to earn a revive), the ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, an app-specific device identifier, your approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. The game does not request permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, and ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as starting, clearing, or failing a level, along with basic device and app information. No names, emails, or Apple IDs are sent.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes levels cleared, best scores, stars, unlocked cats and outfits, trophies, saved revives, and your settings (like sound). Your saved progress itself is never sent to me.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Apple of which items you own, so it can unlock them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and trophies are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Other players may see your Game Center name and scores on those leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can manage it in your device's Settings.

Children's privacy

FlipCat Dash is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information such as names or contact details from anyone, including children, and its ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
